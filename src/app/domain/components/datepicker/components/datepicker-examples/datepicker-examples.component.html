Inline calendar examples

Inline calendar with different configurations: default, range dates, disabled dates, and highlighted dates.

Default


<day-datepicker [type]="'datetime'" [autoclose]="true" [placeholder]="'selected date'"
[formControl]="dateControl"/>

Range dates


<day-datepicker [type]="'datetime'" [autoclose]="true" [placeholder]="'selected date'"
[rangeDate]="true" [formControl]="dateControl1"/>

Disabled dates


<day-datepicker [type]="'datetime'" [disabledDates]="disabledDate" [autoclose]="true"
[placeholder]="'selected date'" [formControl]="dateControl2"/>

Highlight dates


<day-datepicker [type]="'datetime'" [autoclose]="true" [placeholder]="'selected date'"
[highlightDates]="highlightDates" [formControl]="dateControl3"/>

Autoclose or not datepicker

Autoclose


<day-datepicker [type]="'datetime'" [autoclose]="true" [placeholder]="'selected date'"
[rangeDate]="true" [formControl]="dateControl4"/>

Not Autoclose


<day-datepicker [type]="'datetime'" [autoclose]="false" [placeholder]="'selected date'"
[formControl]="dateControl5"/>

Type datepicker examples

Date


<day-datepicker [type]="'date'" [autoclose]="true" [placeholder]="'selected date'"
[formControl]="dateControl6"/>

Datetime


<day-datepicker [type]="'datetime'" [autoclose]="false" [placeholder]="'selected date'"
[formControl]="dateControl7"/>

Time


<day-datepicker [type]="'time'" [autoclose]="true" [rangeDate]="true" [placeholder]="'selected date'"
